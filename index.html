01 · Nek'zali the Soulcoiler

ROTATING WELL TEAMS · INTERRUPTS · ENERGY RACE
Mythic mechanic — Grasping Depths: a Drowned Echo spawns inside the Soulcoil Well, pulling and damaging the entire raid until killed. Send one assigned team into the Well, rotate interrupts, dodge Swirling Spirit beams and kill it quickly. Soul Exhaustion prevents the same team from returning for one minute, requiring three teams. Invoke now interrupts casts and silences interrupted players for three seconds.

Execution Plan

  1. Handle all Heroic mechanics: shield-break Restless Amani, Rend dispels at wall, clear Barrage lane and enter Ignition with no Ritual Burn.
  2. On Grasping Depths, raid stabilizes outside while called Well Team enters.
  3. Inside: kick Drowned Echo, dodge beams and use personal DPS cooldowns. Exit immediately when it dies.
  4. Raid leader calls Team 1 → Team 2 → Team 3; never send Soul Exhaustion players.
  5. At 50%, kill Echoes sequentially and burn corpse piles with Hungering Pyre/Cremation.
  6. Phase 2: Bloodlust, stop casts before Invoke to avoid its silence, and kill before 100 energy.

Tanks

  • 5–6 Hollowing Strikes swap.
  • Boss to coffin clusters.
  • Barrage away from raid.
  • Maintain add control during Grasping pull.

Healers

  • One assigned healer per Well team.
  • External inside team if needed.
  • Cooldown Ignition/Grasping overlaps.
  • Stop casts before Invoke.

DPS

  • Honor Well team rotation.
  • Fixed kick order downstairs.
  • Magic damage on shields.
  • Full burn Phase 2.
Wipes: wrong Well team; missed Drowned Echo kick; Well team hit by beam; add reaches Well; Invoke interrupts a crucial cast; Ritual Burn remains at Ignition.
CALL: Adds/wall/Barrage → Grasping: called team down, kick and kill → rotate teams → 50% corpses → Lust P2 → stop casts for Invoke.

02 · Entombed Sentinels

PROTO-VENOM PAIRS · SPLIT RAID · EXACT-FOUR INTERMISSION
Mythic mechanic — Shifting Protovenom: marked players must touch another marked player to neutralize both. Touching an unmarked player triggers Protovenom Eruption, damaging and knocking back everyone within 10 yards. Establish pair lanes away from the main groups.

Execution Plan

  1. Retain two balanced sides, bosses 40+ yards apart, health synchronized.
  2. When Protovenom appears, marked players identify their assigned partner and meet at the side's marked neutralization point.
  3. Unmarked players clear lanes immediately; marked players do not cross through the raid.
  4. Blood puddles remain at edges; soak Acid droplets and dodge Living Venom return lines.
  5. Intermission Helical Toxins must total exactly four: pair 2+2 or 1+3. Any total above four is lethal.
  6. Return to original room side while tanks exchange bosses so mark stacks fall.

Tanks

  • 40-yard separation.
  • Swap bosses each intermission.
  • Mitigate Empowering Slam.
  • Keep pair lanes clear.

Healers

  • Cooldown late mark stacks.
  • Watch Protovenom movers.
  • Dispel Blighted Blood.
  • Stabilize before Helical solve.

DPS

  • Know Protovenom partner.
  • Clear pairing lanes if unmarked.
  • Balance bosses.
  • Solve exact-four, never improvise.
Wipes: Protovenom collides with unmarked player; partners cross center; bosses too close; puddles block pair point; Helical total exceeds four; health imbalance heals a boss.
CALL: Split/balance → marked pairs meet, everyone else clears → blood edge → droplets/return → exact four → tanks swap bosses.

03 · The Lost Explorers

TIMED CRATES · SPLINTERS · COUNCIL CONTROL
Mythic mechanic — Relic Rupture: breaking a crate applies Splinters raid-wide. A crate left alive for 25 seconds deals additional raid damage. Crates must die on schedule—neither instantly overlapping Splinters nor timing out.

Execution Plan

  1. Keep exactly two bosses together and balance all three health bars.
  2. Assign each crate an owner/caller. Break sequentially under healer timing, collect fish immediately.
  3. Feed ultimate order Gebbo → Nama → Iku at 90–95 energy.
  4. Gebbo: bombs edge, mushrooms preserved, enter as Blast Wave arrives.
  5. Nama: Team A sets 1/3, Team B set 2; never overlap Mighty Thud circles.
  6. Iku: place circles edge and clear opposite types one at a time with cooldown coverage.

Tanks

  • Rotate Nama between targets.
  • Never cluster all three.
  • Swap Shredding Shards.
  • Keep bosses off crate lanes.

Healers

  • Cooldown scheduled Splinters.
  • Call delayed crate if raid low.
  • Cover Iku clears.
  • Top Thud targets.

DPS

  • Kill only called crate.
  • Never allow 25-second timeout.
  • Deliver fish promptly.
  • Honor ultimate soak teams.
Wipes: two crates break together; crate times out; all bosses stack; fish late; overlapping Thuds; multiple Frostfire clears; Shell Spin through raid.
CALL: Two bosses only → crate 1, stabilize, crate 2 → fish order Gebbo/Nama/Iku → execute assigned ultimate.

04 · Vashnik the Malignant

TUMOR AIMING · FOUNTAIN ROUTE · STACKING BURST DOT
Mythic mechanics: every Imbibe spawns Malignant Tumors with 99% damage reduction. Aim Plague Wave through each Tumor to remove Hardened Tumor, then kill it. An add reaching the center applies a stacking one-minute Malignant Burst DoT—normally unrecoverable during progression.

Execution Plan

  1. Use Fire+Shadow → Fire+Blood → Blood+Shadow route unless your strategy specifies otherwise.
  2. Pre-mark Tumor positions and assign Froth targets/backup aimers.
  3. When Plague Froth expires, align one cardinal Plague Wave line through the Tumor without crossing raid.
  4. Call “Tumor broken,” then hard-swap and kill it.
  5. Continue standard add priority: Burning → Clotting → Shrouded; stagger Burning deaths.
  6. Handle infections and Catalytic Bile while keeping all adds from center.

Tanks

  • Exact fountain positioning.
  • Swap each Dripping Fangs.
  • Place boss for clean Tumor line.
  • Prevent add path to center.

Healers

  • Cover Imbibe + Tumor windows.
  • Stagger Caustic Surge deaths.
  • Top Froth aimer.
  • Treat any Burst stack as emergency.

DPS

  • Do not hit hardened Tumor.
  • Swap immediately after Plague Wave.
  • Keep standard add priority.
  • Assigned Bile soaks.
Wipes: Froth misses Tumor; wrong line crosses raid; Tumor remains alive; add reaches center; double Burning death; wrong fountain pair.
CALL: Set fountains → mark Tumor → Froth line through it → call broken → kill Tumor → Fire/Blood/Shadow → nobody reaches center.

05 · Sszorak

14-PLAYER STACK · VIRULENCE SPREAD · FOUR-WAY WINDS
Mythic mechanics: Serpent's Fury targets a player while Sszorak gains rage. At least 14 players must stack within 8 yards to trigger To the Slaughter before 100 rage. The charge applies Virulence; everyone then spreads so visible beams touch nobody. Raging Crosswinds now uses four cardinal directions.

Execution Plan

  1. Read tunnel order and place cysts opposite active winds as on Heroic.
  2. Maintain two alternating Mutilate teams; place Caustic Residue at edge.
  3. Serpent's Fury target enters the fixed raid stack; exactly designated players collapse quickly.
  4. After charge, spread to assigned wedges until Virulence beams expire. A beam hit reapplies Virulence.
  5. For four-way Crosswinds, locate the specifically assigned opposite-direction partner.
  6. Intermission: ride cysts toward center in correct order and burn during Dig In.

Tanks

  • Place Residue at edge.
  • 6-stack Corroding swap.
  • Keep boss aimed for Fury charge.
  • Call Mutilate A/B.

Healers

  • Cooldown To the Slaughter.
  • Track Virulence spread.
  • Cover wind/cyst movement.
  • Watch Mutilate vulnerability.

DPS

  • Know Fury stack membership.
  • Spread to fixed Virulence wedge.
  • Know four-way wind partner.
  • Place cyst exactly.
Wipes: fewer than 14 stack; boss reaches 100 rage; Virulence beam chains; wrong Crosswind partner; cyst misplacement; same Mutilate team twice.
CALL: Winds/cysts → Mutilate A/B → Fury target in, 14 stack → charge → instant wedge spread → four-way partners → cyst sequence.

06 · The Twin Fangs

9-STACK DEATH · CC SHIELDS · TAINTED BLOOD
Mythic changes: nine Eternal Venom stacks kill. Ithraz shields Caustic Globules; apply assigned crowd control to break their shields before soaking. Tainted Blood creates red orbs that must also be soaked before they explode.

Execution Plan

  1. Maintain three Ravenous Feast groups and strict personal stack tracking.
  2. On Caustic Globules, assigned CC players break shields—do not attempt early soaks.
  3. Once shield breaks, assigned low-stack players soak every orb.
  4. Assign Tainted Blood red orbs by marker/sector and soak all before detonation.
  5. Kill Venomous Emergence adds and dodge Corrosive Spit lines.
  6. Drop Coiling Ichor edge; tanks execute Stone Breaker order.
  7. Transitions leave Sanguine Storm puddles while beam rotates—move cleanly into next sector.

Tanks

  • Call Stone Breaker order.
  • Control two-boss cleave.
  • Keep movement predictable.
  • Track own Venom threshold.

Healers

  • Track nine-stack death limit.
  • Cover orb soakers.
  • Cooldown transition puddles.
  • Call emergency Feast priority.

DPS

  • Assigned CC breaks shield.
  • Assigned players soak green/red.
  • Three Feast groups fixed.
  • Kill adds and dodge every line.
Wipes: shielded Globule explodes; red orb missed; ninth Venom stack; wrong Feast group; tank misses Stone Breaker; beam/puddle collision.
CALL: CC shields → soak green → soak red sectors → adds/lines → Feast 1/2/3 → Ichor edge → tank order → rotate with beam.

07 · The Coiled Altar

PURPLE ORB LANES · PERSONAL GHOSTS · GLOOMBOMB BREAK
Mythic changes: Virulent Mutation creates purple orbs that wipe the raid if they touch another orb or survive the phase. Carrying any orb gives 200% pickup vulnerability for 10 seconds. Only each fixated player can see their Manifestation of Dread. Spiteful Soulcoilers have 99% reduction until struck by Gloombomb. Blocking an intermission Fragment applies a raid-wide 20% vulnerability for two seconds.

Execution Plan

  1. Assign normal and purple orb carriers with separate lanes. Purple never crosses any orb.
  2. Rotate carriers around the 10-second vulnerability; never repick early.
  3. Destroy every purple orb before phase end, using tank frontal without clipping another cluster.
  4. Ghost-fixated players alone control their visible ghost by looking away/moving, then looking at it to stop it. Cluster ghosts for Soul Sever.
  5. Aim Gloombomb through Spiteful Soulcoiler to remove protection, then interrupt and kill it.
  6. Intermission soul blockers stagger contacts by more than two seconds so vulnerability does not stack lethally.
  7. Phase 3 layers purple orbs, ghosts, Soulcoilers, mind controls, Guillotine and Nightfall—use a fixed priority call tree.

Tanks

  • Frontals clear assigned objects only.
  • Swap all frontal vulnerabilities.
  • Hold bosses for Gloombomb line.
  • Stack bosses P3.

Healers

  • Track orb-carrier vulnerability.
  • Stagger Fragment damage.
  • Dispel Venomfang.
  • Cooldown P3 object clears.

DPS

  • Stay in assigned orb lane.
  • Personal ghost control.
  • Gloombomb Soulcoiler then kill.
  • Block souls on fixed order.
Wipes: purple orb touches any orb; purple survives phase; carrier repicks with vulnerability; ghost touches player; Gloombomb misses Soulcoiler; Fragments blocked together.
CALL: Purple lanes isolated → rotate carriers → frontals clear → owners herd ghosts → Gloombomb Soulcoiler → stagger souls → repeat all in P3.

08 · Ula'tek

EGG LANES · SYNCHRONIZED DOOMSCALES · INCUBATION SOAKS
Mythic changes: eggs are Hardened before movement and attach Noxious Shell to carriers. Two carriers within three yards cause Noxious Splash and a bad hatch. After carrying, Rancid Yolk increases the next Shell damage by 200% for 20 seconds. Weakened Doomscales gain Revenge when their partner dies. Phase 3 adds Toxic Womb/Wretch and Toxic Incubation streams that must be intercepted in cleared lanes.

Egg & Side Rules

  1. Assign non-crossing egg lanes and carrier rotations. Never reuse a Rancid Yolk carrier during its 20-second window.
  2. Remove Hardened state as required by the strategy, then move each egg alone—three yards minimum between carriers.
  3. Caustic Waves may never cross eggs or incubation lanes.
  4. On split sides, stagger Grasping Fangs breaks and feed cauldrons small eggs before large.
  5. Balance both Weakened Doomscales and kill nearly together; Revenge gives the survivor 100% Haste and damage.

Intermission & Phase 3

  1. Alternate Coil groups exactly; Soul Constrictor prevents consecutive mitigation.
  2. Toxic Womb drops a Wretch that awakens after 20 seconds. Kill before Boiling Venom.
  3. Fester Burst punishes players farther than 10 yards—collapse appropriately.
  4. During Toxic Incubation, assigned players intercept streams in an egg-free lane. Each intercept gives Toxic Burn and spawns Caustic Waves from that player.
  5. Never allow streams to reach Wretch; Mother's Boon reduces damage taken and increases damage done per stack.
  6. Continue Bite soak groups, Purge spread, Circling Prey evacuation and Shrieker control.

Tanks

  • Never lose boss melee contact.
  • Balance Doomscale health.
  • Pick up Wretch immediately.
  • Keep adds in intercept-safe region.

Healers

  • Track carriers and Rancid Yolk.
  • Cooldown synchronized Doomscale deaths.
  • Track Toxic Burn interceptors.
  • Plan second platform break.

DPS

  • Fixed egg lane and rotation.
  • Kill Doomscales together.
  • Assigned Incubation intercept.
  • Kill Wretch before enrage.
Wipes: carriers within three yards; repeat carrier has Yolk; wave hatches eggs; one Doomscale dies early; stream reaches Wretch; interceptor spawns wave through eggs; Wretch reaches Boiling Venom.
CALL: Hardened off → carriers in fixed lanes → never cross → small then big eggs → Doomscales die together → alternate Coils → Wretch → intercept in clear lane → dodge spawned waves.
